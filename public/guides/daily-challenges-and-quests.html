Daily Challenges & Quests Guide

Discovering colors is only half of Infinite Colour Craft. The other half is progression: daily challenges with streaks, quests with pigment rewards, a recipe book tracking all 150+ colors, and a palette builder for saving your favorite sets. This guide explains how each system works and how to get the most out of them.

The daily challenge

Every day the game generates one challenge: discover a set number of colors matching a target — a category like Neon, a rarity like Rare, a hue range, a lightness band, or a raw number of new combinations. Your progress is tracked on the board itself, so you always know how many qualifying discoveries remain. Finish the quota and a reward pigment is unlocked for claiming.

Streaks matter. Completing the challenge on consecutive days builds a streak, and a missed day resets it to zero. If you only open the game for five minutes, spend them on the daily target first — it is the highest-value activity per minute in the game.

Claiming rewards

Completed does not mean claimed. After finishing the daily quota, open the Daily tab and claim the reward pigment, otherwise it sits unclaimed and the day's effort earns nothing. The same applies to quests: finished quests wait in the Quests tab until you collect the reward color into your inventory.

Quests: milestones that pay pigment

Quests are longer-term goals — discover a full category, reach a discovery count, find your first Epic. Each carries a specific pigment reward, often one that is hard to stumble into by blind mixing. Check the Quests tab early in each session: an almost-finished quest should shape what you mix next, since its reward may unlock the next branch of your collection.

Recipe book and palettes

The Recipe Book is your Pokédex: every pigment you have found, organized and searchable, plus a view of how much remains. Use it to spot empty categories and plan mixing sessions. The Palette Builder lets you save groups of colors — practical for artists who want a Cyberpunk Neon set or an Earth-tone kit one click away in the Paint Studio.

A simple daily routine

Open the game, read today's target, mix toward it until the quota fills, claim the reward, glance at nearly-done quests, and only then free-play. Ten focused minutes a day grows a collection faster than an hour of random mixing — and keeps the streak alive.
